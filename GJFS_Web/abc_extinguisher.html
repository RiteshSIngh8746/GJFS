ABC Dry Powder Fire Extinguisher

ABC extinguishers are effective against Class A (ordinary combustibles), B (flammable liquids), and C (electrical) fires.

When to Use:

  • Fires involving paper, wood, cloth (Class A)
  • Flammable liquids like petrol, kerosene (Class B)
  • Electrical equipment fires (Class C)

How to Use:

  1. Pull the pin to break the tamper seal.
  2. Aim low, pointing the nozzle at the fire's base.
  3. Squeeze the handle to release the extinguishing agent.
  4. Sweep from side to side at the fire’s base until extinguished.
